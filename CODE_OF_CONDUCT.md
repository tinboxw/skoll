# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Skoll contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and specific. Good technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not.

## Expected Behavior

- Assume good intent while asking precise questions.
- Critique code, docs, and decisions rather than people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on reproducible facts and acceptance criteria.
- Make space for contributors with different experience levels.
- Respect security reporting boundaries and private disclosure windows.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, insults, or sexualized language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disrupting discussions after maintainers have asked for a reset.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass security, review, or quality gates.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues, request behavior changes, temporarily restrict participation, or ban repeat offenders. Enforcement should be proportional, documented privately where possible, and focused on protecting the project community.
